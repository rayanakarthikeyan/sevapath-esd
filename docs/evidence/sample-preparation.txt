SevaPath - Income certificate preparation
Independent prototype, not a government application

Route: Citizen login
Progress: 3/3 self-declared items

[x] One identity document
A ration card, Aadhaar card or voter ID (EPIC). One alternative from the published form.

[x] Evidence of income
An IT return, payslip, income evidence or affidavit. No payslip? The form lists alternatives; confirm the suitable proof with MeeSeva.

[x] Passport-size photograph
Keep a clear passport-size photograph ready.

Confirm the exact service, current documents, fees and process with MeeSeva. For scholarships, confirm whether a fee-reimbursement-specific income service is required. No application is submitted or approved by this checklist.
